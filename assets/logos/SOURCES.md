# Logo sources

Logos of collaborating institutions and companies, shown in the carousels on
`cv.html` and `publications.html` (and as timeline logos on `cv.html`). They are
the trademarks of their owners and are used only to identify collaborators.

Raster files are downscaled to about 320 px wide. "Commons" is Wikimedia Commons;
"en/es/pt wiki" are files hosted on that Wikipedia. Files marked *light* are white
logos for dark backgrounds; the site draws them dark (`is-light` class, or
`"light": true` in `data/affiliations-manual.json`).
